## 🎮 About the Game

ShipShaper: Falconeer Chronicles is a casual indie ship-design experience developed by Tomas Sala. It entered Steam Early Access on September 2, 2026. Players create historical, fantasy, steampunk, and military vessels through free-form shaping, then test them at sea or export them for 3D printing, modding, and game development.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ShipShaper: Falconeer Chronicles**. A creative-workflow trainer for ShipShaper that streamlines free-form ship construction, prop placement, sailing tests, and design iteration. It is intended for the game's shipbuilding and exploration features rather than combat or conventional resource management.

**Target Audience:** Ship designers, creative sandbox players, modders, 3D-printing hobbyists, indie developers, and players who want faster experimentation with custom vessels.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement is primarily used while inspecting ships and testing them on the water. Use the trainer's movement controls only during sailing tests or scene presentation, and return to normal controls when evaluating the vessel's natural handling.

### Tools & Inventory

ShipShaper does not use a conventional character inventory or resource economy. Its workflow centers on ship forms, props, functional items, saved designs, and export files. The trainer therefore focuses on construction access and iteration rather than money or consumable resources.

### World & Environment

After shaping a vessel, place it in the available world or presentation scenes to inspect its proportions and appearance. Use the trainer's scene controls for positioning, viewing, and photo-mode adjustments without changing the ship's underlying design.

### Combat & Weapons

ShipShaper is a creative shipbuilding experience with no combat system. Combat-related trainer options are not applicable. Sailing tests are intended to evaluate propulsion, hull shape, weight, and general behavior.
